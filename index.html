Preview the fan’s response patterns

01

Resting, facing forward

The fan rests with its blades off and facing forward when a person leaves the area for less than 11 minutes, or stays near the fan for more than 23 minutes.

02

A closer approach

At approximately two feet away, the proximity sensor activates. As someone approaches within two feet, the blades slowly and progressively speed up to express urgency.

03

Attentive to presence and breath

The fan stays attentive while a person speaks, sighs, exhales, breathes, or blows toward it, as well as when they come closer or are too close.

04

Emotion in the rhythm

Laughter, amusement, joy, and happy words bring rhythmic, fast staccato airflow. Sadness, crying, and unhappiness bring a slower, gentle staccato as the blades decrease speed.

05

A suspended, swinging fan

The design uses three or four integrated fans: one side runs while the other is off, then they switch sides to make the unit swing. The fan hangs from one or two cords and needs a cordless motor.
