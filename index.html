Preview sensor and response states (simulation only)

01

Off when the room is empty

Room occupancy is simulated here. A real setup needs a tested occupancy sensor and conservative timeout; no presence, stale data, or sensor fault must request off.

02

Attentive at 2 ft; off inside

The requested behavior is attentive at about 2 ft (0.61 m), with a conservative off response when closer than 2 ft. A hobby sensor is not a safety-rated protective device.

03

Breath carries the poem away

With the microphone deliberately enabled, a breath-like sound dissipates the p5.js poem. The demo's simple loudness detector can mistake other sounds for breath.

04

Emotion in the rhythm

A laugh can request fast, staccato airflow only with an approved compatible motor controller. This pull-chain fan has no confirmed electronic speed interface.

05

Display panel below the fan

The ceiling-hung concept places the display, microphone, and controls together in a panel below the fan. The two-hour irritation cue is modeled as a gentle visual swing; the diagram is illustrative, not a mounting plan.
